How CareSync Transforms Healthcare

A unified platform connecting patients, diagnostic reports, and medical advice.

1. Medical Reports Vault

Never lose paper slips again. Upload past and present blood tests, X-rays, MRI scans, and discharge summaries in PDF or JPG format with date and lab tags.

  • Chronological timeline ordering
  • Categorized by lab, radiology, or ECG
  • Instant full-screen document inspection
2. Clinical Care Plan Builder

Doctors evaluate uploaded records, formulate diagnoses, and prescribe medications with dosage, frequency, and mandatory lifestyle precautions.

  • Multi-drug prescription rows
  • Dietary and physical activity rules
  • Follow-up consultation calendar
3. Interactive Task Adherence

Doctor instructions automatically generate an interactive daily task checklist. Patients mark items as "Done" or "To Do Yet", updating compliance in real time.

  • Real-time adherence percentage meter
  • Morning, afternoon, and night alerts
  • Doctor monitoring dashboard
